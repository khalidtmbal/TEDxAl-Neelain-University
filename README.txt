TEDxAl Neelain University — starter website

PAGES
- index.html: Home
- about.html: About
- register.html: Register your interest

PREVIEW
Open index.html in a browser. The pages link to one another. Google Fonts and remote Unsplash images require an internet connection.

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. The registration form is front-end only. It validates required fields but does not send/store data. Connect a backend or form service before using it to collect real registrations.
2. Replace stock imagery with approved Al Neelain University/event images where available.
3. Add official event date, venue, contact email and social accounts when confirmed.
4. Confirm TEDx branding/license requirements and event naming with TED before publishing.
5. Add privacy-policy wording that matches your actual data collection and retention practices.

To preview locally, simply open index.html. To host, upload all four files (HTML pages, styles.css, script.js) together to your hosting provider.
