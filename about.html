TEDxAl Neelain University is a planned, independently organized TEDx event designed to bring the TED experience to our university community. It creates space for speakers and attendees to exchange perspectives, ask better questions, and explore ideas that matter — on campus and beyond.

TEDx events are independently organized under a free license from TED. The event's final programme, speakers, venue, and date will be announced when confirmed.
